# Your Website — Simple Guide

Hi Ori. This guide is for **you**, not a developer. No coding. Take it step by step.

Your website has a hidden control panel where you can add new projects, upload photos,
and change your text. You reach it by adding **`/admin`** to the end of your web
address, like this:

> **https://your-website.com/admin**

That's the only "technical" thing to remember.

---

## Part 1 — One-time setup (do this once)

You only do this section a single time, to get the website live and give yourself
access to the control panel.

### Step 1 — Put the website online (Netlify)

1. Go to **netlify.com** and create a free account (use "Sign up with GitHub" if you
   have GitHub — easiest).
2. Click **Add new site → Import an existing project** and choose this project's
   repository.
3. Netlify already knows how to build it. Just click **Deploy**. After a minute your
   site is live on a temporary address like `your-site.netlify.app`.

### Step 2 — Turn on the control panel login

1. In Netlify, open your site → **Integrations** (or **Identity**) → enable
   **Identity**.
2. Inside Identity, open **Services** → click **Enable Git Gateway**.
3. Still in Identity → **Invite users** → type **your own email** → send.
4. Check your email, click the invite, and **choose a password**.

That's it. From now on you log in at **your-website.com/admin** with that email and
password.

### Step 3 — Connect your own domain (optional, when ready)

1. In Netlify: your site → **Domain settings** → **Add a custom domain**.
2. Type your domain (e.g. `orilevi.com`) and follow the on-screen steps to update it
   at the company you bought the domain from (GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.).
3. Netlify adds free HTTPS (the padlock) automatically.

## Part 2 — Adding a new project (the main thing you'll do)

This is the workflow you asked for: *"Add this new project, upload images, done."*

1. Go to **your-website.com/admin** and log in.
2. Click **Projects → New Project**.
3. Fill in the fields:
   - **Title** — the project name (e.g. *Maison del Mar*).
   - **Category** — pick from the list (Hospitality, Wellness, Food & Beverage,
     Fashion, Lifestyle, Events, Retreats).
   - **Year** — e.g. *2025*.
   - **Location** — optional (e.g. *Tulum, Mexico*).
   - **Cover image** — click, then **Upload** your cover photo. *Tall/portrait photos
     look best here.*
   - **Order** — a number that controls position. `1` shows first, `2` second, etc.
   - **Description** — one short, confident paragraph.
   - **Gallery images** — click **Add Image** for each photo, upload it, and (nice to
     have) write a short description in *Alt text*. Add as many as you like — the
     website arranges them into a beautiful layout for you automatically.
4. Click **Publish → Publish now** (top of the page).

Wait about a minute and your new project is live on the **Work** page. ✨

> **Tip:** You don't arrange the photos yourself. Just upload them in the order you'd
> like — the site automatically mixes full-width images and side-by-side pairs to keep
> it looking editorial.

### Editing or removing a project

- **Edit:** Projects → click the project → change anything → **Publish**.
- **Delete:** open the project → top-right menu → **Delete**.

---

## Part 3 — Changing your other text

In the control panel, click **Pages**. You'll see three things you can edit:

- **Home / Hero** — your name, the tagline, and the big rotating background photos on
  the front page. Add or replace hero images here.
- **About** — your portrait photo, the headline, and your story.
- **Contact** — your **WhatsApp number**, **Instagram handle**, **email**, and the
  background photo on the contact page.

After any change, click **Publish**.

> **Important — do this early:** open **Pages → Contact** and replace the placeholder
> **WhatsApp number** and **email** with your real ones. (WhatsApp number = country
> code + number, digits only, e.g. `972500000000`.)

---

## Photo tips

- Upload your **good, full-size** photos. The website handles sizing for phones and
  laptops automatically and keeps them looking sharp — you don't need to shrink them.
- **Cover images** look best **portrait** (taller than wide).
- Gallery photos can be any shape — the layout adapts.

---

## A few good habits

- Always finish with **Publish → Publish now**, or your change won't appear.
- Changes take about a minute to go live. If you don't see it, refresh the page.
- You can edit from your phone too — `/admin` works in a mobile browser.

---

## If something looks wrong

- **A photo is broken / grey:** re-open that item in `/admin` and re-upload the image.
- **The demo photos are still showing:** those are placeholders — replace them by
  editing each project and the Home/About/Contact pages with your own photos.
- **Can't log in:** make sure you accepted the email invite and set a password
  (Part 1, Step 2).

That's everything. Add projects whenever you have new work — it only takes a few
minutes. 🤍
