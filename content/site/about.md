Ori Levi is a commercial lifestyle photographer working across hospitality, wellness, food and beverage, fashion and events.

My work lives between commercial and authentic - for brands that want their images to say something, not just look good. Hotels, private homes, restaurants, retreat spaces, fashion labels. The common thread is atmosphere: places and moments with a feeling worth holding onto.

I approach every project as a story rather than a shot list. I spend time in a space before I shoot it - reading the light, the rhythm, the way people move through it, and above all letting myself feel what the place does to me. That's what separates photographs that are technically fine from ones that actually carry what was there.

Based in Israel, available worldwide. I work with private clients, designers, business owners, retreat founders, marketing teams and brands that care about how they're seen.
