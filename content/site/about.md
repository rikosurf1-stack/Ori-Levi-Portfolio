  Ori Levi is a commercial lifestyle photographer working across hospitality,
  wellness, food & beverage, fashion and lifestyle brands.


  My work sits between editorial and commercial — images with the atmosphere of a
  magazine feature and the clarity a brand needs to sell a feeling. Hotels, villas,
  restaurants and wellness spaces all trade on the same thing: the sense of being
  somewhere worth being. My job is to make that sense visible.


  I approach every project as a narrative rather than a shot list. I spend time
  understanding the space, the light and the rhythm of a place before I begin, so
  the photographs feel authentic rather than staged. The result is a coherent
  visual world a brand can use everywhere — from a website and social channels to
  print and press.


  Based on the road and available worldwide, I collaborate with hospitality groups,
  design studios, marketing teams and founders who care about how their brand is
  seen.
